# Nmesomachi-Uche-Ogbuagu-
Realistic views . Social commentary and issues 
Nmesomachi Uche Ogbuagu is a Mass Communication graduate of Babcock University, specialising in Broadcasting, with practical experience in English and Igbo radio presentation, creative writing, content development, audience engagement and media communication. Her work brings together storytelling, research and a thoughtful interest in social issues and the human experience. She has prepared broadcast content, scripts, discussion themes and audience-facing material, and has contributed to media awareness campaigns combining social media, physical outreach, vox pops and interactive sessions. Her professional interests include communications, digital content, public relations, storytelling and media.

Alongside broadcasting and content development, Nmesomachi is interested in writing that invites readers to think critically, consider different perspectives and pay closer attention to the world around them.
